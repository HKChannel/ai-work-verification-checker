# AI Work Verification Checker

A single-page app in one file (`index.html`) with no dependencies. It's the companion tool for an edition of the LinkedIn newsletter **AI in Finance, Done Right**: **"A UK Tax Tribunal Just Called Unchecked AI 'Professional Incompetence.' Every Accounting Firm Should Read Elden v HMRC"**.

## Features
- **Tab 1, Verification gap:** Enter how many AI-assisted documents your team sends outside each month, the claims or citations in each, the share checked against the source today, an assumed error rate (presets: cautious 5% or a benchmark ~20%), minutes to check a claim and reviewer cost. See AI claims a month, likely errors leaving unchecked, review hours to close the gap and the monthly cost.
- **Tab 2, Policy check:** Six controls drawn from the judgment, from a named owner for every AI output to deadline rules. You get a readiness dial, the biggest gaps and a draft AI verification policy you can copy.

The demo figures and error rates are assumptions, not findings from the judgment. This is not legal advice. Everything runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded.
