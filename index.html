AI Work Verification Checker

In Elden v HMRC (January 2026), a UK tax tribunal found that AI-produced case summaries filed by a firm of chartered accountants had not been verified with sufficient care, and called it "professional incompetence." Use this page to estimate how many AI-drafted claims leave your team unchecked, what that could let through, and the review time needed to close the gap.
